This month has been hectic.

If you're in SF [check it out](http://www.wix.com/lounge/san-francisco).

Also been hanging out at [Noisebridge](http://www.noisebridge.net) a lot. They raised $30K through Indiegogo and we're building all kinds of stuff like 3d-Printers and hacking the planet.
